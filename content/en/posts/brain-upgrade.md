_26 October 2026_

You watched 47 reels today. Learned 12 fascinating facts. Saved 6 posts that might change your life.

Congratulations. Your brain has become a very sophisticated squirrel.

Here's the problem: **collecting information isn't the same as understanding something.**

Understanding happens when ideas connect. When something suddenly clicks. When you stop saying “Interesting!” and start saying “Holy shit, that changes how I see things.”

That's what a good book does.

Not because books are magical. Most aren't. Some are just very long LinkedIn posts wearing hardcover jackets.

But a _short, insightful book_ that takes one important question and helps you genuinely understand it?

That's intellectual nutrition.

And perhaps that's why I find [Knowelia](https://knowelia.com/) so interesting. Its little books, called **Knowles**, aren't trying to impress you with how much information they contain.

They're trying to leave you with something far more valuable: a different brain than the one you arrived with.

Go read one.

Your squirrel deserves an upgrade (and my brother some more readers...)

_— Victor Odasnac_
